# Tiny Japanese course lesson video
title: Finding Your Way
episode: 12
scene: street

Alex: すみません。えきはどこですか？ | Excuse me. Where is the station?
Mika: まっすぐいってください。 | Please go straight.
Mika: みぎです。 | It is on the right.
Alex: ありがとうございます！ | Thank you very much!

vocab: えき | eki | station
vocab: まっすぐ | massugu | straight ahead
vocab: みぎ | migi | right
