# Tiny Japanese course lesson video
title: Talking About Family
episode: 7
scene: home

Mika: これはわたしのかぞくです。 | This is my family.
Alex: そうですか。 | I see.
Mika: こちらはあねです。 | This is my older sister.
Mika: こちらはおとうとです。 | This is my younger brother.

vocab: かぞく | kazoku | family
vocab: あね | ane | older sister (your own)
vocab: おとうと | otouto | younger brother
