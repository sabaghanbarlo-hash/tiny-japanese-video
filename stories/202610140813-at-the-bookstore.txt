title: At the Bookstore

Alex: すみません。この ほんは いくらですか？ | Excuse me. How much is this book?
Mika: せんえんです。 | It's 1,000 yen.
Alex: ちょっと たかいですね。 | It's a little expensive, isn't it?

Alex: じゃあ、あの ほんを ください。 | Then, that book, please.
Mika: はい、どうぞ！ | Sure, here you go!
