# Tiny Japanese course lesson video
title: Shopping
episode: 6
scene: store

Alex: これはいくらですか？ | How much is this?
Mika: せんえんです。 | It is 1,000 yen.
Alex: そうですか。これをください。 | I see. I’ll take this, please.
Mika: はい、どうぞ。 | Certainly. Here you are.

vocab: これ | kore | this
vocab: いくら | ikura | how much
vocab: せんえん | sen en | 1,000 yen
