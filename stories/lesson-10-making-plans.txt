# Tiny Japanese course lesson video
title: Making Plans
episode: 10
scene: cafe

Mika: いっしょにコーヒーをのみませんか？ | Would you like to have coffee together?
Alex: いいですね！ | That sounds good!
Mika: さんじにあいましょう。 | Let’s meet at three.
Alex: はい、またあとで！ | Okay, see you later!

vocab: いっしょに | issho ni | together
